---
name: Personalized discovery
description: MAD MAX recommendations should optimize movie-fit and discovery quality, not compulsive engagement.
---

Recommendations should combine explicit ratings, recent viewing, watchlist exclusions, candidate quality, recency, and genre diversity. Keep the feed finite and explainable; personalization is for helping users find a great movie, not trapping them in an opaque engagement loop.

**Why:** The product goal is accurate movie discovery and discussion, while intentionally addictive dark patterns are not appropriate.

**How to apply:** Prefer transparent, bounded ranking with a trending cold-start fallback and preserve user controls when adding discovery features.
