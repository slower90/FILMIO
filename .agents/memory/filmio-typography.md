---
name: FILMIO typography
description: The premium display-font split used across the FILMIO web experience
---

FILMIO uses Space Grotesk for readable futuristic geometric headings and UI display text, while Michroma is reserved for the FILMIO wordmark.

**Why:** Michroma gives the brand its sci-fi identity but becomes too wide and visually heavy when used for every heading.

**How to apply:** Keep display sizes responsive with clamp-style sizing and avoid returning Michroma to long headings or body copy.
