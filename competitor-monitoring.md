# FILMIO Competitor Monitoring Brief

Last updated: 2026-09-02
Alert channel: Email via Google Alerts

This brief tracks the major global and India-focused movie discovery, rating, review, streaming, curation, and ticketing alternatives relevant to FILMIO. It is a lightweight monthly review source of truth, not an exhaustive list of every movie website.

## Competitors tracked

### Global rating and discovery

- [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/) — database, ratings, reviews, recommendations, watch options
- [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/) — critic and audience consensus
- [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/) — film diary, lists, reviews, social taste
- [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/) — critic Metascore and User Score
- [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/) — community metadata, ratings, lists, watchlists
- [JustWatch](https://www.justwatch.com/us/about-us) — streaming availability and unified watchlist

### Curation and regional communities

- [MUBI](https://mubi.com/en/us/about) — human curation, releases, and film criticism
- [FilmAffinity](https://www.filmaffinity.com/us/faq.php) — taste affinity recommendations and ratings
- [SensCritique](https://www.senscritique.com/films) — lists, rankings, and cultural community
- [Douban Movie](https://movie.douban.com/) — China-focused movie ratings and reviews
- [BookMyShow](https://in.bookmyshow.com/) — India-focused discovery, local context, showtimes, and ticket booking

## Monthly review ritual

1. Open the alerts from the previous month and record only meaningful changes.
2. Compare changes across rating trust, discovery, social features, availability, mobile, curation, and creator tools.
3. Ask whether the change is table stakes, a performance improvement, or a genuine delighter.
4. Update the FILMIO opportunity list:
   - canonical FILMIO score and confidence
   - verified-watch and spoiler-safe reviews
   - evidence-based “Why This Film?”
   - functional mobile experience
   - taste compatibility
   - streaming and theatre availability
   - private Cinema OS sync
5. Choose one customer-visible response for the next product cycle and record it in the change log.
