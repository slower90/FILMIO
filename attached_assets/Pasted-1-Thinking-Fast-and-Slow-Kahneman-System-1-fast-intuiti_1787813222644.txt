1. Thinking, Fast and Slow (Kahneman) — System 1 (fast/intuitive) vs System 2 (slow/deliberate)
→ FILMIO mein: casual user ke liye 1-tap star rating (System 1), power user/critic ke liye detailed review editor (System 2). Dono paths honi chahiye, forced nahi.

2. Influence (Cialdini) — 6 triggers: reciprocity, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity, commitment
→ Social proof sabse powerful hoga: "2.3K logo ne ye rate kiya", "tumhare jaise taste wale logo ne pasand kiya". Authority = verified critics/actors ka review highlight karo. Scarcity avoid karo (fake "only 3 left" jaisa kuch nahi — ye trust todta hai).

3. The Social Animal (Aronson) — belonging & identity
→ Fandom-based identity: user apne aap ko "MCU fan" ya "Anime purist" jaisa label kare, similar log dhundhe. Ye FILMIO ke actor-networking feature ko aur powerful banata hai.

4. Predictably Irrational (Ariely) — anchoring, decoy effect
→ Pricing page (Free/Pro) mein anchor sahi rakha hai already. Rating scale bhi anchor hai — 1-10 use karke tum "premium precision" ka anchor set karte ho (5-star wale apps se alag).

5. Blink (Gladwell) — thin-slicing, first 2 seconds decide sab
→ Homepage/first-open experience critical hai — colors, layout, ek nazar mein "ye premium hai" feel aana chahiye (isiliye color decision itna zaroori tha).

6. The Power of Habit (Duhigg) — Cue → Routine → Reward loop
→ Healthy loop: Cue = daily personalized pick notification, Routine = rate/review, Reward = apna rating ek public identity/reputation ban jaye (top reviewer badge). Important: infinite-scroll-jaisa manipulative variable-reward loop nahi banayenge — tumne pehle bhi clearly bola tha ki addictive/manipulative pattern nahi chahiye, aur ye sahi instinct hai.

7. Drive (Pink) — Autonomy, Mastery, Purpose
→ Sabse important framework yahan: log FILMIO pe isliye ruke rahenge kyunki (a) unhe freedom hai kya dekhna/review karna hai, (b) unki mastery dikhti hai (reviewer level/reputation grow hota hai), (c) unka purpose hai — auron ko sahi movie choose karne mein madad karna. Ye 3 mila ke genuine retention banate hain, dopamine-trap nahi.

8. Games People Play (Berne) — hidden social dynamics
→ Comment/review section design karte waqt troll patterns samajhna zaroori — moderation aur healthy discourse encourage karo, toxic "gotcha" games discourage karo.

9. The Happiness Hypothesis (Haidt) — elephant (emotion) vs rider (reason)
→ Product emotionally warm lage (community, belonging) lekin decisions ko rational bhi support kare (clear ratings, no clickbait). Genuine satisfaction target karo, sirf "time spent" metric nahi.

10. The Psychopath Test (Ronson) — manipulation ki extreme understanding
→ Ye actually ek warning list hai — jo tactics psychopaths/dark-pattern designers use karte hain (guilt, fake urgency, exploiting insecurity), unhe FILMIO mein avoid karna hai. Isse hum "kya nahi karna" clearly define kar sakte hain.
