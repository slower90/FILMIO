---
name: Genre-first personalization
description: Home recommendations are organized by actual content genres, not user age brackets.
---

MAD MAX home discovery should use explicit genre or mood pools for personalization. Age brackets are not recommendation categories; any age suitability belongs only to content-rating metadata or a concise badge when available.

**Why:** Age buckets made recommendations feel arbitrary and allowed mismatched titles into sections such as teen picks.

**How to apply:** Build For You from genre-backed queries and keep section titles faithful to the genre IDs used by the query.
